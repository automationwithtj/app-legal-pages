BabiLink Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 26, 2026 | Last Updated: July 26, 2026

Welcome to BabiLink. We are committed to protecting the privacy of parents, caregivers, and children using our baby tracking and growth application.

1. Information We Collect

BabiLink collects data strictly necessary to provide baby tracking, growth monitoring, and vaccine scheduling features:

  • Baby Profile Data: Name/nickname, date of birth, gender, height, and weight entries.
  • Caregiver & Activity Logs: Caregiver role (Mom, Dad, Nanny, etc.), feeding times, diaper logs, sleep duration, and vaccine completion dates.
  • Local Device Storage: All baby logs and care history are stored locally on your device using encrypted Async Storage.

2. How We Use Information

Your data is used strictly to provide in-app features:

  • Displaying daily stats, timeline logs, and growth charts.
  • Tracking pediatric vaccine schedules (Saudi/GCC and WHO guidelines).
  • Calculating Islamic sunnah and milestone reminders.
  • Providing data-aware AI assistant responses when requested.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your or your baby's personal or health data to third parties or advertisers. All baby logs remain under your control.

4. Data Deletion Rights

You can clear all stored baby data directly from the app's Settings menu at any time. You can also request complete account and data removal as outlined in our Account & Data Deletion Policy.
