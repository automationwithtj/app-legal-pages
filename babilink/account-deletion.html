BabiLink Account & Data Deletion

Google Play Data Safety & User Rights Declaration

BabiLink respects your right to complete control over your personal and baby tracking data. In compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies, here is how you can delete your data:

Option 1: Instant In-App Data Reset

1. Open BabiLink on your device.
2. Tap the Settings / Caregiver button in the top right corner.
3. Tap Reset & Delete All Local Data.
4. Confirm deletion. All baby profiles, logs, and caregiver history will be permanently wiped from your device.

Option 2: Direct Support Request

If you have uninstalled the app or need full assistance removing any associated records, email our support team:

Email: tjappssupport@gmail.com
Subject: BabiLink Account & Data Deletion Request

We process all deletion requests within 48 hours and confirm upon completion.
