AZH WMS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 July 2026 · Applies to the AZH WMS Android application (com.automationwithtj.azhwms)

AZH WMS is the mobile companion for the Abu Zinadah Hospital Warehouse Management System. It is an internal operations tool for authorised hospital staff. It is not a medical, diagnostic, or patient-facing app, and it never handles patient records.

1. Who is responsible

The app is developed and maintained for Abu Zinadah Hospital by Talha (automationwithtj). For any privacy question or request, contact tjappssupport@gmail.com.

2. What the app collects

DataWhyWho provides it
Account details — email address, password, full name, assigned role To sign in and to apply the correct access level Created by the warehouse administrator. There is no public sign-up in the app.
Warehouse records — orders, requested items and quantities, requester name, department, receiving and return records, inventory levels, prices, supplier names, medicine expiry batches, invoices These are the operational records the system exists to manage Entered by staff inside the app, or by anyone submitting the public order form
Public order form entries — the name, department and item list typed by the requester, plus a housekeeping access code where required So the warehouse knows who requested what Typed by the requester. No login is needed for this screen.
Activity logs — the acting user's name, role, action, module and timestamp Accountability for stock movements and approvals Recorded automatically when a signed-in user performs an action
AI Assistant usage log — user name, role, AI provider, model, timestamp To show managers who is using the assistant and how often Recorded automatically. The text of your questions and answers is not stored.
The app does NOT collect: analytics or usage tracking, crash telemetry, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, or any patient or clinical information. There are no ads and no marketing tools in the app.

3. Where the data is stored

All warehouse and account data is stored in the hospital's Supabase (PostgreSQL) database and transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Passwords are stored by Supabase Auth in hashed form and are never visible to the app or to other users. Access requires an authenticated hospital account.

4. What is stored on your phone

  • Your sign-in session token, so you are not asked to log in on every launch.
  • Your appearance preference (light / dark / system).
  • A read-only cache of recently viewed warehouse screens (inventory lists, pending order counts) so the app remains readable when the phone is offline. No changes can be made while offline.

This local data is held in the app's private storage area, protected by your device's own encryption and screen lock. Signing out clears your session; the offline cache is replaced the next time the same screen loads online. If you want everything removed immediately, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings.

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with anyone else, and is never used for advertising.

6. Permissions

The app requests network access only. It does not request camera, location, microphone, contacts, or photo-library permissions.

7. Retention and deletion

Warehouse records are retained by the hospital for as long as they are needed for inventory and audit purposes. Staff accounts are created and removed by the warehouse administrator.

To have your account and associated personal data deleted, follow the instructions on the Account & Data Deletion page, or email tjappssupport@gmail.com.

8. Children

The app is a workplace tool intended for hospital employees aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children and is not knowingly used by them.

9. Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, this page is updated and the "Last updated" date above changes with it. Material changes are communicated to hospital staff through the warehouse administrator.
