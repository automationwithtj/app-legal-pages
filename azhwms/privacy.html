Privacy Policy for AZHWMS Mobile

Last Updated: July 12, 2026

The AZHWMS Mobile Companion App ("we," "us," or "our") serves as the mobile client for the Abu Zinadah Hospital Warehouse Management System. We are committed to protecting the privacy of hospital staff and ensuring the security of warehouse transaction data. This Privacy Policy details how we handle user data.

1. Data Collection & Purpose

Because the app is a closed-loop internal hospital system, data collection is restricted to functional warehouse actions:

  • Credentials: The app uses email and password for staff login. Accounts are provisioned directly by the hospital administrator. No self-registration is available.
  • Warehouse Transactions: We collect order details, quantities, receiving inputs, department logs, and inventory updates submitted by staff to sync with the central database.
  • Verification Codes: Housekeeping staff verify access codes via the WMS web API to submit clean orders. No personal identity is captured or processed for these checks.

2. Cloud Sync & Database Security

Unlike offline-first apps, this client integrates with the hospital database:

  • Secure Supabase Connections: All warehouse data is synced directly over HTTPS to the secure database servers. All transactions follow Row Level Security (RLS) policies based on the active user profile's role.
  • No Analytics or Marketing: We collect no usage analytics, tracking data, crash telemetry, or marketing data. There are no ads or third-party tracking SDKs included.

3. Local Storage

The App uses the following local storage subsystems on your device:

  • AsyncStorage: Stores secure session tokens (encrypted key pairs) to keep you logged into your warehouse account and persist your dark/light theme setting.

4. Data Control & Deletion

  • Account Deletion: Staff accounts are managed by the administrator. Users can initiate account deletion via the WMS web dashboard or contact support.
  • Local Session Cleanup: Pressing "Log Out" inside the app will instantly delete all cached credentials, tokens, and active session states from your device.
