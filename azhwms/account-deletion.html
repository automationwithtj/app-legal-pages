AZH WMS — Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: 23 July 2026 · App: AZH WMS (com.automationwithtj.azhwms)

AZH WMS is an internal tool for Abu Zinadah Hospital. Accounts are created by the warehouse administrator — there is no public sign-up. This page explains how to have an account and its associated personal data deleted.

How to request deletion

  1. Email tjappssupport@gmail.com from the email address the account uses, with the subject "AZH WMS account deletion request".
  2. Include the full name on the account so it can be identified.
  3. You will receive a confirmation once it is done. Requests are actioned within 30 days, normally much sooner.

Hospital staff may alternatively ask the warehouse administrator, who can remove the account directly from the system's user-management screen.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Sign-in account — email address, password, full name, role Deleted permanently. The account can no longer sign in.
Appearance preference and offline cache on the phone Removed when the app is uninstalled or its storage is cleared.
AI Assistant usage entries linked to the account Deleted together with the account.
Warehouse records the person created or approved — orders, receiving, returns, inventory changes, and the activity/audit log entries naming them Retained by the hospital. These are business and audit records of hospital stock movements, kept for inventory accountability and regulatory audit as long as the hospital requires. They are not used for any other purpose.
If you want a copy of the personal data held about you before deletion, ask for it in the same email and it will be sent to you.
