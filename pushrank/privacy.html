Privacy Policy for PushRank

Last Updated: July 11, 2026 — App Version 1.0.0

PushRank ("we," "us," or "our") is a global push-up leaderboard app. We collect the minimum data needed to run the leaderboard and streaks, and we are committed to protecting it in accordance with our security protocols. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect and why.

1. Account & Sign-In

PushRank does not require an email address, phone number, or password. When you claim a username, we create an anonymous account (via our backend provider, Supabase) tied only to a random device credential. We collect:

  • Username you choose (3–20 characters).
  • Country (optional) — shown as a flag next to your name on leaderboards.

We do not collect your real name, email, phone number, or precise location.

2. Workout Data

Each time you log a set, we store the rep count, duration, the counting method used (nose-tap, light sensor, or — in a future update — AI camera), and the date/time. This powers your personal stats, your daily streaks, and your position on the leaderboard.

3. What's Public on the Leaderboard

Your username, country flag, and total rep counts (Today / Week / All-Time) are visible to every PushRank user on the public leaderboards. Your individual workout sessions (exact times, durations) are private and never shown to other users — only the totals are public.

4. Where Your Data Lives

Data is stored on Supabase (hosted PostgreSQL, Mumbai region). Row Level Security is enabled on every table: you can only read or change your own account and workout records directly — the public leaderboard totals are served through a separate, restricted process that only exposes username, country, and total reps, never your raw session history. Basic pace and daily-volume checks run on our server to keep the leaderboard fair; they only look at rep counts and timing, not any personal data.

5. AI Camera Counting (Not Yet Available)

PushRank will offer an optional AI-camera rep-counting mode in a future update. This policy will be updated to describe exactly how camera data is processed before that feature is turned on for any user. It is not active in the current version, and no camera or image data is collected today.

6. Ads & Third-Party Services

The current version of PushRank shows no ads and uses no analytics or advertising SDKs. If ads are added in a future update, this policy and the app's Play Store Data Safety section will be updated first.

7. Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account, username, streaks, and rank at any time via Profile > Reset account in the app. This action is irreversible — because sign-in is anonymous, we have no way to recover a deleted account.

8. Children's Privacy

PushRank is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
